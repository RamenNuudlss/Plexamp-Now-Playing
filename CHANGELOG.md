# Changelog

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-07

First public release.

- 23 themes with 83 styles: Ultrablur, Slim bar, Island, Spotlight, Minimal, Lower third, Vinyl, Cassette, CD, Polaroid, Ticket, Receipt, Retro Amp, Terminal, Car stereo, Split-flap board, Pixel RPG, Neon sign, Synthwave, Aero, Brutalist, Lyrics and Visualizer.
- Album colors from Plex UltraBlur, ten palettes, theme colors or custom hex codes.
- Loudness-driven visualizer and motion from Plex's audio analysis; synced karaoke lyrics from the Plex server.
- Style Studio with live previews, obs-websocket sending and shareable style codes.
- Named presets (saved in `presets.json`), managed from OBS or the Studio, with Preset 1–10, next/previous preset and next/previous theme hotkeys.
- Smooth exit-and-enter when the look changes while the overlay is on screen.
- Render size up to 3× for sharp overlays on 1440p and 4K canvases.
- One-click Plexamp visualizer capture with blend modes and fades.
